## 1. Executive summary

**Result under the frozen classification tree: C6.** Substantive reading (Opus Review B, adopted): **non-replication.**

- The pre-specified test (10 cells = 2 displacement signs × 5 horizons, session-level dependence-aware inference, Holm-10
  in VAL, then Holm over the VAL-confirmed cells in a one-shot HOLDOUT) confirmed **three** cells in VAL:
  up-displacement→short at 5 min (+4.6 bp) and down-displacement→long at 10 min (+11.1 bp) and 30 min (+18.7 bp).
- In the one-shot HOLDOUT (2025-04-01 → 2026-09-29, 6,452 events, 369 sessions, MDE 5.7–8.5 bp) **none of the three
  confirmed**: up/h5 reversed (−2.0 bp, p 0.94); down/h10 +1.7 bp (p 0.24); down/h30 +4.1 bp (p 0.14).
- **Net of the equal-weight market**, HOLDOUT down-displacement reversion is ≈ 0 (R_ex +0.4 / +0.8 bp at h10/h30, p > 0.4). A
  stock-specific reversion after extreme downside displacement **was present in TRAIN (R_ex +4 … +6 bp per event, cluster
  p ≈ 0.001) and weakly in VAL, and absent from 2025Q3 onward** [POST-PRIMARY, descriptive].
- **Costs:** statutory round-trip ≈ 4.5 bp + slippage (2 × 2.75 bp base) ≈ 10 bp. **All ten HOLDOUT net-of-cost cells are
  negative** at every slippage scenario tested; no cell reached the economic gate.
- **Robustness (post-primary, disclosure only):** see §20. **Not broad:** the VAL confirmations depend on raw returns with
  session weighting (they do not survive the market-excess or event-clustered versions), and the decay is dated
  ≈ mid-2025, inside HOLDOUT.

> What the data establish: an extreme intraday displacement from VWAP was followed by a *market-relative* reversion in
> 2023 – mid-2025 that is inconsistent with zero in TRAIN, and that **did not replicate out-of-sample**. What they do not
> establish: any persistent, economically meaningful, single-stock reversion after extreme VWAP displacement.

## 2. Research question

Does extreme intraday displacement of an individual stock from its session VWAP exhibit statistically significant and
economically meaningful subsequent mean reversion? An **empirical phenomenon test**, not a strategy: no sizing, portfolio, stops,
indicators or confluence. No RFA was run before or during the experiment, and RFA played no role in deciding whether to test it.

* The closure ledger (`RESEARCH_CLOSURE_LEDGER_2026-09-29.md`) records **M1c as C2 — EMPIRICALLY UNKNOWN**: the only M1c artefacts are

  cross-sectional or daily; MRLC conditions on sweeps and volume; RELIANCE `vwap_E1` is an *execution variant*, not a test of VWAP

## 5. Hypothesis

**H1.** Mean forward return **in the reversion direction** (long after negative displacement from VWAP, short after positive) is
> 0 at each of five horizons (5/10/15/30/60 min), separately for each displacement sign. **H0:** ≤ 0. A significant *negative*
mean (continuation) is a distinct finding, reported but never counted as support. Confirmation is defined by the frozen rule in §8/§17.

## 8. Exact protocol

Machine-readable protocol: `docs/reports/research/vwap_extreme_reversion_protocol.json` (v1.0, sha256 in the freeze record). Summary:

* **Decision** at the close of 1m bar *k*, *k* ∈ slots 59…294 (clock **10:15 – 14:10**); entry = **open of bar *k*+1**; exit = **close of bar *k*+H**, H ∈ {5, 10, 15, 30, 60} min. One event set serves every horizon.
* **Event:** first bar per (name, session, side) with |z| ≥ c[bucket]; sides balanced by construction; ≤ 2 events per name-session.
* **Primary statistic:** R_H = sign × (exit/entry − 1) × 10⁴ bp (sign = −1 after up-displacement, +1 after down-displacement), gross.
* **Inference unit:** the session (events averaged within a session). One-sided Newey–West (Bartlett, lag ⌊4(n/100)^{2/9}⌋) p; 95% circular block-bootstrap CI (10,000 reps, seed 20260930); event-level pooled mean with two-way (name × session) clustering as secondary.
* **Family:** 10 cells (2 sides × 5 horizons); **Holm-10** in VAL (α = 0.05, one-sided, mean > 0). HOLDOUT is tested **only** on VAL-confirmed cells (Holm over that set), with the pre-registered delay-2 microstructure qualifier for H ∈ {5, 10}.
* **Classification tree** (first match wins; TRAIN and all robustness never enter): C4 defect trigger → C5 nothing VAL-confirmed → C8 clean HOLDOUT-confirmed + economic gate → C7 clean HOLDOUT-confirmed → C6 VAL-confirmed, HOLDOUT positive-signed but not confirmed → C5 otherwise. Implemented in `classify.py` (11 tests).
* **C-vocabulary caveat:** the repo uses C0–C6 in the closure ledger but defines C7–C9 nowhere textually; the mapping (C5 criterion tested and failed; C6 positive but incomplete; C7 HOLDOUT-confirmed; C8 economically confirmed; C9 replicated across independent windows) is inferred from ledger usage and stated in the protocol before any result existed.
* **Governance:** freeze record hashes the protocol, nine modules and the membership file; outcome measurement refuses to run on any mismatch; HOLDOUT is one-shot (marker file) and requires the VAL cells file.

## 9. VWAP construction

Cumulative session VWAP **through the decision bar inclusive**: VWAP_k = Σ_{i≤k} TP_i·V_i / Σ_{i≤k} V_i over valid bars from 09:15, with
TP = (H+L+C)/3 (primary; close-weighted VWAP is a robustness variant). Missing/synthetic/zero-volume bars carry zero weight and are never imputed. A
name-bar is eligible only if valid bars ÷ elapsed bars ≥ 0.90 **through the decision bar** (information available at the decision time only). Session start 09:15
(slot 0); the decision window ends 14:10, so the session end/CAS carry-forward region (15:15+) and the closing auction never enter. Tests assert VWAP against brute force
and its invariance to future bars.

## 10. Displacement normalization

z = (C_k − VWAP_k) / (VWAP_k · σ_prior · √D), D = k + 1 elapsed minutes since 09:15. σ_prior = mean over the **prior 20 sessions** (strictly before today; ≥ 15 finite values)
of the name's per-session RMS of consecutive valid 1m log-returns (≥ 200 pairs) — **today's data never enters**. Robustness-only: a range-based prior scale ×√(D/375); raw displacement in bp.

## 11. Event definition

At each qualifying bar: positive displacement (z > 0) is tested for subsequent *downward* reversion (short); negative displacement for *upward* (long).
Event timestamp = the decision bar close; entry price = next-bar open; exit = close of bar *k*+H (H bars held from the entry open). Overlap: same-name same-side events cannot overlap
(one per session); opposite-side events are both kept; cross-name overlap is handled by session-level aggregation. Duplicates: first qualifying bar only (non-declustered "all bars" is a robustness variant).
Events near the close: none generated after 14:10, so every horizon exits by 15:10. Warm-up: the first 80 sessions build state and produce no events.
A missing entry or exit bar makes that horizon's outcome NaN (never imputed); NaN shares are reported per cell (max ≤ 0.1%, cap 2%).

## 12. Threshold definition

c[b] = the q-quantile (q = **0.99**) of |z| pooled over **all eligible universe name-bars of the prior 60 sessions in the same 15-minute decision bucket b** (16 buckets over D ∈ [60, 295]); symmetric in sign;
today and future sessions never enter. q was chosen by an **outcome-free** rule before any forward return existed: among {0.99, 0.995, 0.999} the q whose TRAIN event incidence (share of eligible name-sessions with ≥ 1 event) is
closest to 10% (M1c ledger precedent) → incidence 9.06% (q = 0.99; 0.995 → 5.17%; 0.999 → 1.75%); 3,668 TRAIN events (≈ 12.9 per session, up/down 1,827/1,841). Re-run unchanged after the Opus-A σ-count fix.

## 13. Horizon definition

5 / 10 / 15 / 30 / 60 minutes, pre-declared and unchanged. Adjusted for nothing: the decision window (10:15–14:10) was chosen so that the *longest* horizon still exits by 15:10, which (i) keeps the event set identical across horizons, (ii) keeps every era identical
(pre-/post-CAS), (iii) avoids the 15:12 MIS square-off and the 15:15+ carry-forward region. Extra horizons (20, 45) were run **post-primary** as disclosure only.

| Risk | Control | Evidence |

## 17. Statistical tests

* **Primary:** one-sided Newey–West on the session series (Student-t, n−1 df). AC1 of the session series is reported in the cell CSVs; lag = ⌊4(n/100)^{2/9}⌋ = 4–5.
* **Interval:** 95% circular moving-block bootstrap of the session-series mean (block = NW lag + 1, 10,000 reps).
* **Secondary (pre-registered):** event-level pooled mean with two-way clustered SE (`cluster_t`, `cluster_p_one` in `results/primary_*_cells.csv`).

## 28. Limitations

1. **Store universe.** "PIT F&O members the store carries" — 75–78% of PIT F&O names through Feb 2025, 87–100% after; the store's name list looks chosen on a later universe (survivorship-shaped, plausibly conservative for intraday reversion). Composition shifts across stages (≈139 → 154 → 197 mean names) and TRAIN-names-only HOLDOUT reproduces the full-universe HOLDOUT [POST-PRIMARY].
2. **Sample length.** 920 sessions (3.75 years) in one market microstructure era; C9 (independent replication) is unreachable — no other window or venue exists on this data.
3. **Estimand.** Raw absolute return includes market drift/reversal; R_ex assumes β = 1 against an equal-weight benchmark that includes the event names (conservative on many-event days). A β-adjusted leave-own-name-out benchmark was **not** computed.
4. **Session-weighting** (the pre-registered primary) over-weights low-event sessions; session-weighted net is not implementable P&L.
5. **κ = 2.75 bp** is borrowed from a different construct; true slippage at extremes may be larger (5 bp scenario reported; conclusions unchanged).
6. **Results/earnings days** were not tagged (no verified announcement calendar on this path).
7. **Break dating is post-primary and descriptive**; any external explanation (F&O expansion Dec 2024, participant mix, CAS Aug 2026) is narrative, not a finding.
8. **Prior exposure:** the 2023–2026 1m equity store was read by earlier, different constructs (ISD F1/F4, MRLC, DayType, JEV, RELIANCE); none tested VWAP displacement. ISD's SEALED window (2026-01 →) belongs to ISD.
9. **Certified-artefact defect found, not fixed:** ISD `pit_universe` F&O flag under-counts eight names (no effect here; recomputation used).

## 29. Exact empirical conclusion

**Classification.** Under the frozen tree: **C6** (VAL-confirmed cells; none HOLDOUT-confirmed; HOLDOUT point estimates positive in two of three). **Substantive reading (adopted from Opus Review B; Review C to confirm below): non-replication — empirically failed out-of-sample at the tested resolution, and not economically meaningful.** The label must never be quoted without the non-replication clause.

**What the data establish (pre-specified primary; POST-PRIMARY where marked):**

1. Under the frozen protocol, the hypothesis "extreme displacement from session VWAP is followed by reversion in the contrarian direction" was **not confirmed out-of-sample**: 0 of the 3 VAL-confirmed cells confirmed in the one-shot HOLDOUT (2025-04-01 → 2026-09-29; 6,452 events; 369 sessions; MDE 5.7–8.5 bp at h10/h30). The up-displacement h5 VAL result **reversed** (−2.0 bp).
2. Net of the equal-weight market, HOLDOUT reversion after down-displacement is **≈ 0** (R_ex +0.4/+0.8 bp at h10/h30, p > 0.4), and the HOLDOUT 95% intervals sit below the TRAIN estimates. The positive raw HOLDOUT points are market-level.
3. **A stock-level, market-relative reversion after extreme downside displacement was present in 2023-05 → mid-2025** (TRAIN R_ex ≈ +4 … +6 bp per event, two-way-cluster p ≈ 0.001; VAL weaker) **and measures ≈ 0 for five consecutive quarters from 2025Q3** [POST-PRIMARY, descriptive]. Up-displacement never showed a stable reversion.
4. **No cell is economically meaningful.** The statutory round trip alone is ≈ 4.5 bp and a plausible all-in cost is ≈ 10 bp (κ = 2.75) to ≈ 15 bp (κ = 5); every HOLDOUT gross cell is ≤ +8 bp at best and **all ten HOLDOUT net cells are negative at every κ** (session-weighted and event-weighted). Even the best TRAIN market-relative event mean (+5.8 bp) is below plausible cost.
5. The apparent in-sample strength is **estimator-dependent**: VAL confirmations vanish under market-excess or event-clustered inference, are carried by singleton-event sessions, and depend on session equal-weighting; a non-declustered event definition produces large session-mean "significance" whose event-weighted mean is ≈ 0 and whose net is negative.

**What the data do not establish:**

* That the mechanism is *absent*: a market-relative reversion existed in-sample for ~2 years; whether its disappearance is a regime/microstructure change (post-hoc narrative — F&O expansion Dec 2024, participant mix, CAS Aug 2026) or sampling is not determined.
* Anything about other anchors, horizons, universes (non-F&O, pre-2023, other venues), threshold rules, or the persistence-defined events beyond disclosure; no other independent replication window exists (C9 unreachable).
* Results-day / earnings effects (not tagged) and a β-adjusted leave-own-name-out benchmark (not computed).

**Closure statement.** The **construct as specified** — own-volatility-scaled displacement of the close from cumulative typical-price session VWAP, extreme by a rolling pooled q = 0.99 threshold, first crossing in 10:15–14:10, next-open entry, five horizons, PIT F&O names the store carries — is **closed as empirically not replicated out-of-sample and not economically meaningful** (frozen label C6; substantive reading C5-equivalent non-replication). This is a **construct-scoped** closure: it does **not** close M5c (VWAP anchoring) or M1c (extreme reversion) as mechanisms, does not license "VWAP reversion doesn't exist", and does not extend to any other anchor, universe, horizon or definition.
