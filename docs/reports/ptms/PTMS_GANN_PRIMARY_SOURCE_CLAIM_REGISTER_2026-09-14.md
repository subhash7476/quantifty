## 0. Executive summary

1. **Three Gann-authored works were searched in full text**, with close reading of every passage cited:
   - *Truth of the Stock Tape* (1923);
   - *Wall Street Stock Selector* (1930), bound with the 1923 book in the same scan, with appended
     forecast and service material;
   - *The Tunnel Thru the Air* (1927), a novel.

   **Gann's later works were not available** from trusted open sources: *New Stock Trend Detector*
   (1936), *How to Make Profits in Commodities* (1941 and later), *45 Years in Wall Street* (1949),
   and the courses and letters of 1930s–1955. This is the register's central provenance limit.
2. **The two stock books contain no geometric vocabulary.** Across ~41,600 OCR lines, "angle",
   "degree", "geometr", "logarithm" and "anniversar" occur **zero** times. "Square" occurs once, as
   the chart shape "square tops". **Gann angles, the 1×1, the Square of Nine and price-time squaring
   are absent from the primary stock corpus inspected**, not merely under-specified in it.
3. **Every angle-scale rule located is a commodity convention, and every one is secondary-sourced**
   (cents per day/week/month for grains, points per day for cotton). No equity scale rule was found
   in any source. **Fixed-scale 1×1 answer: `NOT FAITHFULLY TESTABLE FROM PRIMARY SOURCES`** (§15).
4. **Gann's verified time rules are absolute calendar-duration norms, not relative comparisons.**
   *Wall Street Stock Selector* pp. 48–52 and 83–84 state:
   - buy active stocks after a reaction of 2–3 weeks; most will not react more than 3–4 weeks before
     the main trend resumes;
   - strong stocks seldom react into the 2nd month; once a move runs into the 2nd month, watch the
     3rd or 4th month;
   - the 3rd or 4th move is the culminating period;
   - fast moves culminate in 6–7 weeks;
   - an advance or decline of 85–100 points "in a short time" means watch for a top or bottom;
   - wait for a 2–3 day halt at a top or bottom before acting.

   **A rule that a correction outlasting the largest prior correction signals a trend change
   ("time overbalance") was NOT FOUND.** In these books "overbalance" refers only to supply, demand
   and volume. GO-2's relative form is a modern reinterpretation. The **absolute reaction-duration
   rules are a faithful, if under-specified, alternative** (§16).
5. **The anniversary claim is verified, at monthly resolution and anchored on a stock's own turn.**
   *Wall Street Stock Selector* p. 55: after a bottom in a given month, the most important date is
   the same month one year later, explicitly "not the calendar year", when one should watch for
   "at least a change in the minor trend". Also verified: 3rd/6th/9th/12th-month checkpoints,
   day-of-month dates, and market-wide fixed date bands.
   - **A 360-day market anniversary was NOT FOUND.** Gann's own chart arithmetic counts a year as
     365 days.
   - The 360 days / 360 degrees equivalence appears only in the novel, as a character's Biblical
     exegesis: `UNCERTAIN` (§17).
6. **Gann's own words weigh against a common-parameter cross-section.** Stocks "have habits", must be
   studied "individually and not collectively", should not be expected to follow their group unless
   their own chart shows it, and each moves "according to its individual time limit" (§13).
7. **Register: 68 claims.** **VERIFIED 36 · UNCERTAIN 4 · SECONDARY-ONLY 10 · NOT FOUND 17 ·
   CONTRADICTORY 1.**
8. **Decision (§22):**
   - **Arm 1 (faithful, price-time scope):**
     - GT-1 in its verified monthly form;
     - the weekly/monthly reaction-duration rules;
     - the 3rd/4th-move and 6–7-week culmination rules, with the joint 85–100-point clause.

     All are faithful but under-specified, with the mechanization declared as an assumption.
   - **Arm 2 (Gann-inspired):**
     - GA-3/GS-5;
     - GO-2 relative-duration form;
     - GS-3;
     - GT-2 time symmetry (a qualitative primary anchor exists);
     - GT-7 duration recurrence.
   - **Arm 3 (generic):** GA-2/GS-4, and GT-4 applied to a stock cross-section.
   - **Reject from the Gann battery:** the angle, square and Square-of-Nine families, and GT-3.
   - Several **verified Gann rules are not price-time** (three-close rule, greatest reaction, even
     figures, day-of-week and day-of-month dates, per-stock month seasonality). Their scope is an
     operator decision.

## 17. THE PIVOT-ANNIVERSARY FIDELITY QUESTION

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Did Gann use anniversary dates? | **Yes, verified** ([WSSS] p. 55): the same calendar month one year after a stock's bottom, explicitly not the calendar year-end (ANN-01) |
| 360 days, primary support? | **Not found** in the stock books (ANN-05). The 360 equivalence appears only in fiction, as Biblical exegesis (ANN-06, UNCERTAIN). Where the stock books count days in a year, they use **365** (TIM-11) |
| 365 / 365.25? | The anniversary rule itself states neither number. It is **"the following August or one year later"**, i.e. calendar month and year, so the number-of-days question does not arise at the resolution Gann gives |
| Must the anchor be a major pivot? | The example anchors on a bottom from which "the trend continues up", which implies a trend-significant bottom. **No operational definition** (SWG-05) |
| Date, window or reversal? | **A month-level window** in which to watch for "at least a change in the minor trend", possibly lasting one to three months. Not a guaranteed reversal and not a day-precise date. Separate market-wide date bands (ANN-04) are said to mark reversals if a top or bottom forms around them |
| Calendar vs trading days? | **Calendar months**, for this rule (ANN-09) |

### Separating the calendar kinds

The brief asks to distinguish ordinary calendar seasonality, the anniversary of an event, and the
360-degree interpretation. **The primary source does not separate them.** On adjacent pages Gann
endorses four calendar kinds:

1. **Per-stock calendar-month seasonality** (ANN-03).
2. **Pivot anniversaries at month resolution** (ANN-01), plus 3/6/9/12-month checkpoints (ANN-02).
3. **Market-wide fixed date bands** from a "permanent cycle" (ANN-04), plus fixed **day-of-month**
   (TIM-06) and **day-of-week** (TIM-05) dates.
4. **Duration checkpoints from a turn**: 3rd week, 6th–7th week, 2nd/3rd/4th month (TIM-03, 04, 09).

The 360-degree interpretation is **absent** from the stock books.

Consequences:
- A faithful GT-1 test is the **monthly** pivot-anniversary rule.
- Distinguishing it from calendar seasonality is a legitimate *empirical design* question. **Gann
  offers no basis for calling only one of these "the Gann effect".**
- The fidelity review's **360-vs-365.25 contrast has no verified primary basis** and is not a
  Gann-discriminating test (§24).

## 20. HISTORICAL CAUTION

1. **Attribution.** [TST], [WSSS] and [TTA] are published under W. D. Gann's name by Financial
   Guardian Publishing Co. with Gann copyright notices. No attribution dispute was found or
   investigated. [TTA] is a novel: its statements belong to a fictional narrative and characters,
   and its Foreword says the book conceals a secret "clothed in veiled language". **Nothing in it is
   treated as a stated market rule.**
2. **Edition and binding of the scan.** The DLI item binds [TST] (1923) and [WSSS] (1930) in one
   volume; the scan's first leaf says the new book begins after page 185. It also carries appended
   matter under the WSSS running head, paginated ~1–46, including Gann's 1929 Annual Stock Forecast
   (signed November 23rd, 1928) and service and course descriptions. [WSSS]'s contents list
   "Appendix I i–xvi", which **does not match** the observed arabic pagination. The appendix may
   belong to a particular printing or binding. **Its bibliographic identity is UNCERTAIN; its text
   is in Gann's first person.** Whether the copy is a first printing is not established.
3. **Later editions.** Later reprints (Lambert-Gann; Martino) exist and may differ editorially. None
   was read, and no comparison was made.
4. **OCR.** The DLI OCR is visibly corrupt in places ("loth", "nth", "i8", "g:|ins", fractions
   rendered as "^" or "54"). Page numbers come from running heads and may be ±1. **Numeric values
   read from OCR (prices, reaction sizes) are uncertain.** No figure in this register enters any
   computation.
5. **Online quotations.** "When price and time square, change is inevitable" (SQR-01), "time
   overbalances" (TOB-03), the 1×1 as "one cent per day/week/month" (1X1-02), Square-of-Nine rules
   (SQ9-01 to 05) and squaring the range (SQR-02) are **NOT VERIFIED IN PRIMARY SOURCE**.
6. **Tool mediation.** Secondary-source content reached this register via tool-generated search and
   fetch summaries (§1.3) and may be mis-paraphrased. It is used only as a pointer.
7. **Copyright.** Quotations are limited to short excerpts from public-domain works; secondary
   (modern, copyrighted) material is paraphrased only.
8. **Search method and residual risk.** The two stock books were searched by keyword and by a scan
   of every upper-case section heading, and every passage cited was read in context. They were
   **not read line by line in full**.
   - A first draft of this register **missed** the time-rule sections of [WSSS] pp. 44–58 and
     83–95 (now §2.12). They were found by the heading scan and a duration-phrase scan before
     commit.
   - That miss is recorded here because it shows the method's failure mode. **Further verified
     claims may exist in unscanned prose.**
   - The absence findings (angles, degrees, geometry, logarithms, anniversaries, squares, 360-day
     counts, overbalance) rest on exhaustive string search and are robust **to OCR errors only
     where the OCR renders the word intact**.

## 21. Failure-mode check

The chain this register supports is **primary claim → precise interpretation → explicit
assumptions → testable construct**. It supports that chain for:

- **GT-1 monthly anniversary.** Primary (ANN-01/02) → month-level window after an anchor turn →
  assumptions: anchor detector, outcome for "minor-trend change" → construct.
- **Absolute reaction-duration rules** (TIM-03/04). Weeks or months of reaction in active or strong
  stocks → assumptions: activity filter, reaction start, calendar mapping, outcome → construct.
- **Culmination rules** (TIM-01/09/10). Assumptions: move segmentation, "fast", "short time",
  nominal-point translation.
- **Non-price-time rules:** greatest reaction (TOB-04), three-close rule (TOB-10), even figures
  (PRB-04), date bands and calendar dates (ANN-04, TIM-05/06).

It **blocks** the popular-formula route for:
- the 1×1 and angles;
- Square of Nine;
- squaring price and time;
- 360-day time counts;
- relative time overbalance;
- *k*-day swing charts;
- 50% retracement.

For each of these, no primary claim was found in the inspected corpus.

**Watch for "rescue by translation".** Every faithful time construct above states its magnitudes in
nominal points or calendar units for 1920s NYSE stocks. Translating them to percentages, σ, or
NSE sessions is necessary to test them. **That translation must be declared as an assumption**,
never presented as Gann's rule.

## 22. FINAL EXECUTIVE DECISION

### Arm 1 — Faithful Gann

- **Price × time (programme scope)**, all *faithful but under-specified*, with mechanization
  declared:
  1. **GT-1, monthly pivot-anniversary form** (ANN-01), with the 3/6/9/12-month checkpoints of the
     same passage (ANN-02).
  2. **Absolute reaction-duration rules** (TIM-03 weekly, TIM-04 monthly).
  3. **Culmination rules:** 3rd/4th move (TIM-01), 6–7-week fast move (TIM-09), and 85–100 points in
     a short time (TIM-10). TIM-10 is the only joint price-time clause and carries the heaviest
     translation assumption.
- **Faithful Gann rules outside price × time** (operator decides scope):
  - three-consecutive-closes trend rule (TOB-10) — directly operational;
  - greatest-reaction watch and stop distance (TOB-04);
  - even-figure resistance (PRB-04) — nominal, CA-vulnerable;
  - per-stock calendar-month seasonality (ANN-03);
  - day-of-month and day-of-week dates (TIM-05/06);
  - 2–3 day halt and 3-week time stop (TIM-02/07) — execution rules;
  - fixed annual date bands (ANN-04) — **market/index level only**.

### Arm 2 — Gann-inspired modern reinterpretation

**Not to be cited as evidence for or against Gann.**
- GA-3 / GS-5 (self-scaled 1×1 / ratio square; mean-of-swings form per the fidelity review).
- GO-2 relative-duration ("time overbalance") form — until *How to Make Profits in Commodities* and
  *45 Years in Wall Street* are read.
- GS-3 diffusive square.
- GT-2 time symmetry (qualitative anchor TIM-08; the equality or ratio is modern).
- GT-7 duration recurrence (qualitative anchor TOB-08; the estimator is modern).
- Any common-parameter cross-sectional aggregation of a Gann rule (contrary to XS-01 to 03).

### Arm 3 — Generic market properties

- GA-2 / GS-4 normalized angles.
- N-period range breakouts weighted by range length (TOB-07/TIM-11 — Gann endorses, not
  Gann-distinctive).
- GT-4 fixed dates **applied to a stock cross-section** (a market calendar event, per the veneer
  test).

### Reject from the Gann battery

- GA-1, GA-4, GA-5 (angles: not faithfully testable, §15).
- GS-1, GS-2 (literal squares).
- GN-1 to GN-4 (Square of Nine, §18).
- GT-3 (circle-division day counts, not found).
- GT-5 (long cycles, not testable).
- GO-4 (astrology).

### Not enough evidence (hold; reopen only if later primary texts are read)

- GT-1 day-precise / 360-day form.
- GO-1 range divisions.
- GO-5 *k*-day swing charts.
- GT-6 age / listing anniversaries.

1. **Acquiring the unread primary works** through lawful, provenance-safe access (library loan,

   - "fast" and "short time" (TIM-09/10);
