With no row carrying open or close outside [low, high], a zero-range row forces O = C = H = L, so Garman-Klass is exactly zero there rather than negative. That is the case the feature build must handle: `log(GK / median)` is undefined at zero. These are circuit-locked or untraded sessions, and the pre-registered treatment is to floor GK at each stock's 1st percentile of its own non-zero GK before the log — pinned here, before any fitting.
