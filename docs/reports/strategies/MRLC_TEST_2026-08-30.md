# MRLC-Test Backtest Results (v2) - 2023-01-02 to 2026-08-28, + 2012-2022 extension

**Rule set:** price ≥ threshold below the 25-day average (prior-day closes) +
liquidity sweep (close below the recent 10-session low on ≥ 2× usual volume, then
close back above within 3 bars). **News guard:** signals within 3 sessions after a
≥ 8% single-session crash are skipped (earnings/event proxy). Buy at next bar's
open. Stop = sweep low − 2× ATR (1.5× shown). Sell 50% at the 25-day average, rest
with break-even then 2× ATR trailing stop. 20-session time stop. Delivery fees,
era-accurate. Rs 1,00,000 fixed per trade.

## 11. Caveats (read before trusting anything)

- **Survivorship (2023-26 window):** only today's ~196 stocks. The 2012-2022
  extension removes this for the daily variant (point-in-time symbols).
- **Ideal fills:** exits at exact stop/target prices; slippage table (0-20 bp)
  shows the construct survives up to 20 bp per side on the strong cells.
- **News guard is a proxy** (≥ 8% one-day crash → skip 3 sessions). It is NOT a
  real news feed; earnings-adjacent moves under 8% are not caught.
- **Fixed target:** the profit target is the 25-day average as of the signal day.
- **2012-2022 extension specifics:** CA-adjusted prices, pre-2020 stamp duty uses
  the Maharashtra-representative 0.01% assumption, NSE retail-tier transaction
  charge. Stops/targets in % terms on adjusted prices.
- **Selection caveat:** the 10%/15% cells and the news-guard threshold were
  examined across 3 charts × 4 thresholds × 2 stops. The extension and the
  time-split are consistency checks, not fresh pre-registered holdouts.
